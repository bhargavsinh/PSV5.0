About

Free Repository of Pushti Sampraday Granthas

The main objective behind building up the common repository or collection of Pushti Sampraday Granthas is to disseminate the true principles of Jagad Guru Shreemad Vallabhacharya Mahaprabhuji by making the Original Granthas of Pushtimarg available to all the vaishnavas through the Internet media. This will lead to easy and fast availability of Pushtimargiya Granthas to all Vallabhiya Vaishnavas and thus enabling them to understand and implement the principles of our Acharya in its true sense.

This repository contains the original granthas of JagadGuru Shreemad Vallabhacharyaji Mahaprabhuji, Shree Gopinath Prabhucharans, Shree Vitthalnathji Prabhucharans and other Acharyas. The Repository contains the collection of Granthas in Sanskrit, Braj, Hindi as well as Gujarati Languages. All the granthas are freely downloadable** in PDF format. This portal is purely Non-Profit and meant to only serve the Pushti Sampraday. All the materials uploaded on this site are free.
