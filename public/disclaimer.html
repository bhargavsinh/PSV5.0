1. Nature of this service

Pushti Sahitya is a free, non-profit digital repository intended for preservation, discovery, and study of Pushtimargiya granthas and related literature. It is provided as a static website and as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) for offline access to cached pages and resources where available.

This portal is not the official website of the Puṣṭi Sampradāya and does not claim institutional authority over doctrine, initiation, seva, or sampradāya administration.

For spiritual practice and doctrinal questions, please consult qualified teachers and primary sources within the tradition.
