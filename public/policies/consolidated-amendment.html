Consolidated Policy Amendment

Last updated: September 2026 · Amended with stricter enforcement · Site Vβeta 5.0

Document type: Consolidated Policy Amendment with Stricter Enforcement

Effective date: 17 September 2026

Applies to: All use of the Pushti Sahitya website, PWA, offline caches, linked materials, and related services operated under this domain.

1. Purpose of this Amendment

This Consolidated Amendment strengthens enforcement across Acceptable Use, Terms & Conditions, Copyright & IP, Content Usage, and Security policies. Where this Amendment conflicts with an earlier policy wording, this Amendment prevails.

2. Zero-tolerance prohibitions

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate technical blocking, evidence preservation, and referral to competent authorities without prior notice:

  • Unauthorized scraping, bulk downloading, or automated harvesting of site content, metadata, or linked corpus at a volume or rate that harms availability or bypasses published limits.
  • Attempts to circumvent client-side or server-side protections, including but not limited to disabling security controls for the purpose of unauthorized extraction.
  • Injection of malware, XSS payloads, CSRF abuse, path traversal, cache poisoning, or any attempt to compromise the static host, service worker, or users’ devices.
  • Commercial republication, paywalled redistribution, or sale of materials obtained from this repository without written permission from the relevant rights holders and site operators.
  • Misrepresentation of this site as the official Puṣṭi Sampradāya authority, or impersonation of operators, ācāryas, or institutions.
  • Use of site content to train commercial AI / ML models for sale or public redistribution without explicit written licence.
  • Harassment, threats, or abusive communications directed at operators or community members via published contact channels.

3. Stricter enforcement measures

  1. Immediate access restriction — IP ranges, user-agents, or clients associated with abuse may be rate-limited or blocked without advance notice.
  2. Evidence retention — Technical logs reasonably necessary to investigate abuse may be retained for enforcement and legal compliance.
  3. Takedown and link removal — Links or pages may be removed or disabled pending investigation of credible rights or safety complaints.
  4. Cooperation with law enforcement — Unlawful activity may be reported; lawful process will be complied with.
  5. No duty to prior warning — For severe or ongoing abuse, operators are not required to issue a warning before technical countermeasures.
  6. Repeat violators — Persistent violators may be subject to longer-term blocks and refusal of future correspondence except formal legal notices.

4. Permitted use (unchanged intent, clarified boundary)

Personal study, sincere sādhanā-related reading, non-commercial academic citation with attribution, and good-faith research remain welcome. Fair use / fair dealing, where applicable under governing law, is respected within its legal limits — it is not a licence for wholesale copying or commercial productization.

5. Copyright & corpus

Site design, code, and original descriptive text are protected. Underlying grantha texts and external PDFs remain subject to authors’, editors’, publishers’, and communities’ rights. Reporting alleged infringement: admin@pushtisahitya.org with precise URLs and claim details.

6. Security research

Good-faith vulnerability reports are welcome at the same address. Testing must not degrade service, access non-public data, or pivot to third-party systems. Out-of-scope: social engineering of individuals, physical attacks, or DoS.

7. Grievance & contact

Policy or content grievances: admin@pushtisahitya.org. See also the Grievance page. This portal is not the official website of the Puṣṭi Sampradāya.
