Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Read with Consolidated Policy Amendment · Site Vβeta 5.0

Amendment: Stricter enforcement rules in the Consolidated Policy Amendment (September 2026) prevail where applicable.

Overview

Pushti Sahitya is a free, non-profit digital repository of Pushtimargiya Granthas. This Privacy Policy describes how the website handles information in connection with your use of the site.

This website is primarily static. It does not operate user accounts or collect personal profiles for registration.

Information we may process

Technical and log data typically collected by web hosts and content-delivery networks (for example IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps) may be processed for security, reliability and performance.

If you contact us by email (for example admin@pushtisahitya.org), we process the content of your message and your email address solely to respond.

If you use an optional subscription or contact form that may be present, the fields you submit are processed for the stated purpose of that form.

Cookies and similar technologies

Essential cookies or local storage may be used for Progressive Web App (PWA) caching and basic site functionality.

See the separate Cookie Policy for more detail. We do not use advertising trackers as a core feature of this archive.

How we use information

To operate, secure and improve the website; to respond to enquiries; and to comply with legal obligations where applicable.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Hosting, security and infrastructure providers may process technical data as needed to deliver the service.

Retention

Contact emails are retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry. Technical logs are retained according to host and security requirements.

Your choices

You may contact us at admin@pushtisahitya.org regarding privacy questions. Browser settings allow control of cookies and local storage.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website evolves. The “Last updated” date above will change when material revisions are made.
