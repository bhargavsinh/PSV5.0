Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Amended with stricter enforcement · Site Vβeta 5.0

Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), as amended September 2026, defines mandatory rules for all visitors so the archive remains available and trustworthy. It must be read with the Consolidated Policy Amendment.

Allowed

Lawful browsing, searching, reading, and academic citation with attribution.

Respectful contact via published addresses for genuine queries, corrections, or rights notices.

Strictly prohibited

  • Compromising security, injecting malicious code, or disrupting availability (including intentional overload).
  • Automated harvesting, bulk mirroring, or scraping that imposes unreasonable load or seeks to rebuild the corpus off-site without permission.
  • Circumventing technical protections for unauthorized extraction or redistribution.
  • Distributing spam, malware, or unlawful material through or in connection with the site.
  • Impersonating operators or misrepresenting affiliation with the Puṣṭi Sampradāya.
  • Commercial AI training use of site materials without written licence.

Enforcement (stricter)

Violations may result in immediate rate-limiting or blocking, retention of relevant technical logs, removal of links or access paths, refusal of further service to repeat abusers, and referral to authorities where unlawful conduct is suspected. Prior warning is not required for severe or ongoing abuse.
